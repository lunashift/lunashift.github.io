## Design Context

### Users
Women in perimenopause, mostly 40 to 55, browsing on a phone or laptop after hearing about the app from a friend, a clinician or TestFlight. They want to know two things fast: what the app does, and whether their symptom data stays private.

### Brand Personality
Warm, private, calm. Never clinical, never a wellness cliche, never alarmist. Editorial rather than app-store loud. Honest about limits: a tracking tool, not a medical device.

### Aesthetic Direction
Warm cream and terracotta editorial page with a premium product-shot feel. Lora headlines and Poppins body are client-mandated. Real iPhone captures only, no fake UI. One accent (terracotta / blush in dark). Light and dark, both first-class. Anti-references: purple SaaS gradients, glassmorphism everywhere, heavily tilted floating phones.

### Design Principles
1. The phone is the hero: every screenshot sits in a believable titanium frame, upright, lit, with depth.
2. Motion tells the story: reveals on entry, parallax on scroll, nothing decorative that loops.
3. Privacy is a feature, given a whole section and plain words.
4. Honest copy. No diagnosis claims, no fake numbers.
5. Both themes tested before shipping.
