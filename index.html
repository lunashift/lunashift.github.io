Support

Questions, a problem, or need to manage your subscription? Everything is on the support page, or email lunashiftsupport@gmail.com.

What it does

  • Quick-log hot flashes, night sweats, sleep, mood, brain fog and more
  • Record HRT doses and set gentle reminders
  • Scan a supplement label with the camera to log it
  • See patterns across your symptoms and doses over time
  • A weekly read in plain language, not charts you have to decode
  • Bring in sleep, heart rate, activity and weight from Apple Health, if you choose to
  • Export a clean summary for your GP or specialist

Luna Shift helps you track and describe your own symptoms. It is not a diagnosis of menopause or its causes, and it is not a substitute for medical advice.
